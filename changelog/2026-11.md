No image-field ignore, object-label bypass, changed scope or relaxed verification was required.
- **Change:** update ADR 0010, architecture and operational status with final enforced evidence and the observed resolution of tenant image-normalization drift; retain the historical incident/root cause above.
- **Cost impact:** none. **Rollback:** documentation-only change; admission rollback remains a reviewed GitOps revert restoring both warn and Ignore.
